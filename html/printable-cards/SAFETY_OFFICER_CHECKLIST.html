DEDICATED SAFETY ROLE NON-PARTICIPATING OBSERVER

Drill Safety Officer Checklist

Authority to Halt Training at Any Moment
Mandate: When an officer is not present, you are the department's front line against preventable injuries. You do not handle hoses, raise ladders, or swing tools during live reps. Your eyes stay 100% on the firefighters, the apparatus, and the ground.
1. Pre-Drill Safety Scan (Before Reps Begin)
  • PPE Inspection: Pants over boots, coat zipped/velcroed, hood tucked under collar, helmet chinstrap snugly fastened under chin, structural/extrication gloves on, safety glasses worn.
  • WorkSafeNB Reg 91-191 Jewellery Rule: Verify 100% of rings, wristwatches, and neck chains are removed before donning gear.
  • Ground Hazards: Check bay apron and driveway for black ice (salt/sand ready), trip hazards, loose cribbing, and traffic safety.
2. Active Surveillance During Live Reps
  • Ergonomics & Lifting: Watch for members lifting with backs instead of legs during ladder carries, bottle swaps, or hose rolls.
  • Pinch Points: Fingers on ladder beams (not rungs); hands behind hydraulic tool safety guards; body positioned outside tool kick zones.
  • Pressurized Lines: Ensure members do not straddle uncharged hose lines; warn of nozzle reaction kick; confirm backup firefighter is anchored behind nozzleman.
  • Air & Heat Exhaustion: Minimum 4,000 PSI before starting reps; watch for hyperventilation, dizziness, or fatigue. Water/electrolytes ready.
🛑 THE EMERGENCY STOP AUTHORITY
If you see a slip, dropped tool, missing glove, coupling leak, sudden fatigue, or immediate hazard:
SHOUT: "STOP! STOP! STOP!" OR "FREEZE!"
Everyone must cease movement, idle down throttles, and stand in place until cleared.
3. Post-Drill Safety Clearance & Reset
  • Ensure members doff gear in a well-ventilated area to minimize particulate exposure; wipe skin with decontamination wipes.
  • Inspect tools for cracks, hydraulic leaks, or structural fatigue before stowing back in brackets.
  • Confirm no injuries occurred. If any member sustained a minor strain or scratch, log it immediately on the department incident report.
