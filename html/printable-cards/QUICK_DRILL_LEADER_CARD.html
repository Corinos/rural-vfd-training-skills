POCKET REFERENCE LAMINATE & KEEP IN CAB

Quick Drill Leader Card

"I'm Running Training Tonight" (No Officer Required)
The Golden Rule: You do not need to give a lecture. Your role is simply to hold the sheet, read the objectives, stage the gear, keep time, and guide the crew through practical repetitions.
STEP 1: GATHER THE CREW & SET ROLES 3 MINUTES
1. Call the room to order at 19:00 (7:00 PM).
2. Assign Drill Safety Officer: Hand them the Safety Officer card. Remind them: "Your only job is watching for hazards and calling STOP if needed. No physical reps while on safety watch."
3. Pick Tonight's Drill: Check the 52-week schedule or pull the next sheet from the binder. Read Objective and Safety Warnings aloud.
STEP 2: TOOL STAGING & RADIO CHECK 7 MINUTES
1. Direct crew to pull the specific tools and apparatus listed on the drill sheet.
2. WorkSafeNB Reg 91-191 PPE Check: Bunker gear, boots, gloves, flash hood tucked under collar, helmet chinstrap fastened. All rings, watches, and jewellery OFF.
3. Switch all handheld radios to local tactical channel (e.g. Tac 1). Perform a quick radio check.
STEP 3: PHASE 1 – THE SLOW WALKTHROUGH 10–15 MINUTES
1. Walk through the evolution step-by-step with the sheet in your hand.
2. No stopwatch. Half speed only. Have an experienced member demonstrate.
3. Ask: "How does our truck differ from the textbook?", "Where is this valve on Pumper 1 vs Tanker 2?"
STEP 4: PHASES 2 & 3 – LIVE REPETITIONS & SCENARIO 25–35 MINUTES
1. Split crew into pairs (Buddy System). Rotate through 2–3 repetitions so everyone gets hands-on time.
2. Experienced members coach newer members.
3. Phase 3 Challenge: Add realistic stress (gloved hands, darkened bay, reversed hoods, or timed flow).
4. Remind everyone: Anyone can yell "STOP!" if something is unsafe.
STEP 5: TAILGATE DEBRIEF & TRUCK RESET 10 MINUTES
1. Gather at the tailboard of the primary truck. Ask the 3 Debrief Questions:
  • "What went smooth and fast?"
  • "What snagged or slowed us down?"
  • "If this was 02:00 AM on a snowy back road, what would surprise us?"
2. Complete the Truck Reset Checklist (Hose repacked, SCBA refilled to 4,500 PSI, water topped up).
3. Have all members sign the Attendance Roster.
