DRILL 20
Firefighter Survival
NFPA 1001 Level 2 (JPR 5.3.1, 5.3.3) / WorkSafeNB Reg 91-191

Mayday Transmission & Firefighter Self-Rescue

⏱️ Time: 45–60 min 🚒 Apparatus: Any Engine/Rescue 🦺 PPE: Full Structural Turnout Gear & SCBA 👥 Roles: Drill Leader (Facilitator) | Safety Officer (Watcher) | Firefighter Pairs

🎯 Objective & Rural Fireground Value

Every firefighter must be able to recognize a personal fireground emergency, transmit a crisp MAYDAY declaration over the radio using the LUNAR format, silence their PASS device during transmissions, follow the GRAB LIVES survival checklist, and execute a Reduced Profile SCBA Maneuver to escape through 16-inch on-center wall studs.

📦 Tools & Apparatus Setup
  • Full SCBA on air for all participants.
  • Portable radios on training channel.
  • Wall stud prop: 2x4 studs spaced 16 inches on-center (or between two ladders).
  • Entanglement prop (simulated wire box: slack wire cables, BX wire, Romex strung in a frame).
⚠️ Safety Watch-Outs & STOP Rule
  • Mask Seal Integrity: During the reduced profile maneuver, watch that facepieces are not twisted, dislodged, or pulled off by wall studs or wire snags.
  • Panic Management: If a member becomes stuck in the entanglement prop, do not let them flail. Coach them to breathe calmly.
🛑 THE STOP RULE: Shout "STOP!" immediately if an SCBA regulator uncouples or if a member experiences true airway compromise.
Phase 1: Calling the Mayday & The LUNAR Report (15 Minutes) Slow Dry Run
  • When to Call a Mayday:
    • Lost, disoriented, or separated from your crew.
    • Trapped by structural collapse or tangled in wiring.
    • SCBA failure, out of air, or low-air alarm sounding without an exit found.
    • Fall through floor or roof.
  • The Transmission Protocol:
    • Depress radio PTT button: "MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY! Engine 1 Interior with a Mayday!"
    • Await IC acknowledgment: "Command copies Mayday, go with your Mayday."
    • Deliver the LUNAR report:
    • L – Location: "Division 2, Side Bravo, rear bedroom."
    • U – Unit: "Engine 1 Attack Team."
    • N – Name: "Firefighter Smith."
    • A – Air / Assignment: "Search team, 1,200 PSI remaining."
    • R – Resources Needed: "Ceiling collapse, pinned under joists, need RIT and irons."

DRILL 20: Practical Evolutions & Tailgate Debrief

Firefighter Survival • Practical Application
Phase 2: The GRAB LIVES Survival Actions (15 Minutes) Buddy Coaching
    • G – Gauge: Check cylinder pressure.
    • R – Radio: Call Mayday (LUNAR).
    • A – Activate PASS: Turn on PASS device manually.
    • B – Breathing: Drop to skip/box breathing to conserve air.
    • L – Low: Stay on the floor where air is coolest.
    • I – Illuminate: Point flashlight beam straight up toward the ceiling.
    • V – Volume: Bang a hand tool against the floor/wall rhythmically (3 taps).
    • E – Exit Search: Crawl along walls or follow hose line ("Smooth bump-bump to the pump").
    • S – Shield Airway: Protect regulator and facepiece from heat.
Phase 3: The Reduced Profile Maneuver (16" Studs) (20 Minutes) Timed / Stress
  • Interior firefighter trapped in a room with no exit except an interior partition wall.
  • Breach drywall with Halligan or axe between two 16" studs; clear sheetrock from both sides.
  • Loosen your right shoulder strap on the SCBA harness completely.
  • Unbuckle your waist belt and chest strap.
  • Slip your right arm out of the harness strap. Swing the SCBA pack off your back, holding the left shoulder strap firmly with your left hand.
  • Push the SCBA cylinder through the 16" studs ahead of you (or hold it along your left ribcage).
  • Turn your body sideways, duck your head through, and slide through the studs like a swimmer.
  • Once clear, slip right arm back in, tighten shoulder strap and waist belt, and verify facepiece seal!

    ---

⚠️ Common Pitfalls & Fireground Hazards
  • Waiting too long to call Mayday: Waiting until your low-air bell rings before calling Mayday gives the RIT team only 2–3 minutes to reach you. Call early!
  • Screaming over your PASS alarm: The PASS alarm must be temporarily silenced while pressing the PTT button to transmit LUNAR, then re-activated immediately.
  • Disconnecting regulator from facepiece: Never remove your regulator inside a toxic atmosphere, even if air is running low!
🗣️ 5-Minute Tailgate Debrief
  1. "How long did it take to deliver the LUNAR report under stress?", "Did anyone snag their regulator hose during the stud crawl?"
✅ Apparatus Reset Checklist
  • SCBA harnesses inspected for torn stitching or bent buckles.
  • PASS devices reset and switched off.
  • Cylinders refilled to 4,500 PSI.
  • Attendance roster signed.
