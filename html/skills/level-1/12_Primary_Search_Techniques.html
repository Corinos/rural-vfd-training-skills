DRILL 12
Search & Rescue
NFPA 1001 Level 1 (JPR 4.3.9) / WorkSafeNB Reg 91-191

Primary Search Techniques & Oriented Search

⏱️ Time: 45–60 min 🚒 Apparatus: Any Engine/Rescue 🦺 PPE: Full Structural Turnout Gear & SCBA 👥 Roles: Drill Leader (Facilitator) | Safety Officer (Watcher) | 2-Firefighter Search Teams

🎯 Objective & Rural Fireground Value

Every firefighter must be able to conduct a rapid, disciplined Primary Search in an unfamiliar structure under zero-visibility conditions, maintaining continuous orientation using either a perimeter Wall Search (Left/Right Hand) or a Tool-Assisted Oriented Search, locating a victim and communicating findings without becoming disoriented.

📦 Tools & Apparatus Setup
  • Apparatus bay, meeting room, or storage area configured with simulated residential furniture (chairs, tables, pallets, mattresses).
  • Rescue mannequin or weighted dummy (50–150 lbs).
  • Flash hoods reversed over SCBA facepieces to simulate zero visibility (smoke).
  • Hand tools: Halligan bar, 6-ft pike pole / NY hook, flashlight, Thermal Imaging Camera (TIC).
⚠️ Safety Watch-Outs & STOP Rule
  • WorkSafeNB 51.6(2) Compliance: No firefighter searches alone. Partners must maintain voice, visual, or physical contact at all times.
  • Trip & Snag Hazards: Searchers must stay low (crawling or tripod position). Watch for head-strikes against overhead framing or tables.
🛑 THE STOP RULE: Shout "STOP!" immediately if searchers lose orientation, if a member experiences real panic, or if someone steps off a simulated ledge/stairwell.
Phase 1: Search Philosophy & Techniques (10 Minutes) Slow Dry Run
  • The Tripod / Crawl Position:
    • One knee on the floor, one foot forward with boot out in front, head up, reaching forward with hand/tool.
    • Feeling the floor ahead before putting weight down prevents falling through burnt floorboards or down open stairwells!
  • Oriented Search vs Perimeter Search:
    • Wall Search: One partner maintains hand/foot contact with the exterior wall (Right-Hand or Left-Hand pattern), sweeping room perimeter and beds.
    • Oriented Search (Preferred Modern Method):
    • Oriented Firefighter (Team Lead): Stays at the doorway of the room with the TIC and flashlight. Monitors air, hallway conditions, and orientation.
    • Searcher: Moves into the room, sweeps the space using a hand tool to extend reach by 6 feet, sweeps around and under beds, returns directly to the oriented firefighter's voice and light at the door.

DRILL 12: Practical Evolutions & Tailgate Debrief

Search & Rescue • Practical Application
Phase 2: Slow Walkthrough & Sounding the Floor (15 Minutes) Buddy Coaching
  • Pair teams up.
  • Practice entering a room:
    • Sweep the threshold immediately inside the door (victims often collapse right behind doors!).
    • Chock the door open so it cannot latch behind you.
    • Sweep sweeping arcs with the halligan or pike pole handle across the floor, under furniture, and along walls.
    • Listen periodically: Pause for 3 seconds, call out "FIRE DEPARTMENT! ANYONE HERE?", and listen for groans, coughing, or tapping.
Phase 3: Zero-Visibility Scenario (25 Minutes) Timed / Stress
  • Reverse flash hoods over facepieces. Search teams start at the exterior "doorway".
  • Team must:
    • Communicate search pattern: "Engine 1 entering, conducting Left-Hand search!"
    • Search room systematically, locating the simulated victim.
    • When victim is found, call out loudly: "VICTIM FOUND! BEDROOM 1!"
    • Formulate extraction plan (which exit is closest).
    • Drag victim back along the wall to the entry door.
  • Debrief time elapsed and air consumed (PSI check).

    ---

⚠️ Common Pitfalls & Fireground Hazards
  • Leaving the wall without a reference tool: If both searchers drift into the center of a smoke-filled room, disorientation occurs in seconds. Stay anchored to a wall, line, or your oriented partner!
  • Passing doors without checking: Skipping a closed door leaves a fire or a trapped victim behind you.
  • Searching too fast / missing under beds: Children and pets hide in closets, under beds, and behind curtains. Sweep completely under furniture before moving past.
🗣️ 5-Minute Tailgate Debrief
  1. "Did the team maintain communication during the search?", "How much air did you consume during 10 minutes of crawling?"
✅ Apparatus Reset Checklist
  • Mannequins and furniture returned to staging areas.
  • Hand tools wiped clean and stowed in apparatus mounts.
  • SCBA facepieces cleaned, disinfected, and dried.
  • Spare cylinders refilled.
  • Attendance roster signed.
