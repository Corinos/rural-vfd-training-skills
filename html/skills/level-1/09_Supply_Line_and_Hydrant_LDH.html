DRILL 09
Water Supply
NFPA 1001 Level 1 (JPR 4.3.14, 4.3.15) / WorkSafeNB Reg 91-191

Large Diameter Hose (LDH) & Hydrant / Supply Lays

⏱️ Time: 45–60 min 🚒 Apparatus: Pumper / Engine 🦺 PPE: Full Structural Turnout Gear & Gloves 👥 Roles: Drill Leader (Facilitator) | Safety Officer (Watcher) | Hydrant Firefighter & Driver/Apparatus Crew

🎯 Objective & Rural Fireground Value

Every firefighter must be able to execute a Forward Supply Lay using 4-inch or 5-inch (100mm/125mm) Large Diameter Hose (LDH) with sexless Storz couplings, connect to a pressurized municipal hydrant or dry hydrant, flush the hydrant, make the connection with a hydrant gate valve, and charge the supply line without water hammer.

📦 Tools & Apparatus Setup
  • Pumper with LDH supply bed (minimum 200–300 ft loaded).
  • Hydrant kit/bag: Hydrant wrench, 2.5" gate valve, 4"/5" Storz-to-steamer adapter, rubber mallet, spanner wrenches.
  • Station training hydrant or simulated hydrant marker.
⚠️ Safety Watch-Outs & STOP Rule
  • Snag & Flail Hazard: When laying LDH from a moving apparatus, ensure the hydrant firefighter stands firmly in the safety zone (behind the hydrant or off the road curb) and never steps into the loop of paying-out hose.
  • Water Hammer: Never open or close a hydrant or master intake gate rapidly. Minimum 30 seconds to open; minimum 30 seconds to close!
  • Storz Locking Latch: Ensure the locking lever on the Storz coupling snaps shut. If not locked, the coupling can twist and decouple violently under 150 PSI!
🛑 THE STOP RULE: Shout "STOP!" immediately if the paying-out hose catches on a firefighter's boot or gear.
Phase 1: The Hydrant Bag & Storz Coupling Mechanics (10 Minutes) Slow Dry Run
  • The Storz Advantage: Sexless couplings (quarter-turn twist). Align the locking lugs, push firmly together, and rotate 1/3 turn until the spring-loaded lock clicks.
  • The Hydrant Kit Inventory:
    • Hydrant wrench (pentagonal socket).
    • 4" or 5" Storz-to-thread adapter.
    • 2.5" gate valve (allows a second line to be connected later without shutting the hydrant down).

DRILL 09: Practical Evolutions & Tailgate Debrief

Water Supply • Practical Application
Phase 2: The Forward Lay (Catching the Hydrant) (20 Minutes) Buddy Coaching
  • Dismounting & Wrapping the Hydrant:
    • Apparatus stops 15–20 feet past the hydrant.
    • Hydrant firefighter steps off apparatus with hydrant bag, grasps LDH coupling, and pulls 10–15 feet of hose off the tailboard.
    • Wrap the hose completely around the barrel of the hydrant in a secure loop.
    • Stand with a boot firmly planted on the crossed hose to anchor it against the ground.
    • Signal the driver with a loud voice and raised arm: "LAY THE LINE! GO GO GO!"
    • As the apparatus drives forward at 8–10 km/h, the hose pays out of the bed smoothly.
  • Hydrant Preparation & Connection:
    • Unwrap the hose from the hydrant barrel.
    • Remove the steamer port cap using the hydrant wrench.
    • Flush the Hydrant: Crack the operating nut on top 2–3 turns until water flows clear of gravel and rust, then close.
    • Thread the 4"/5" Storz adapter onto the steamer port and tighten with spanner.
    • Thread a 2.5" gate valve onto one of the side ports (closed position).
    • Connect the LDH Storz coupling to the adapter; verify locking latch snaps shut.
Phase 3: Charging the Supply Line & Air Bleeding (15 Minutes) Timed / Stress
  • Await radio confirmation from the pump operator: "Engine 1 is ready for hydrant water. Charge the supply line!"
  • Rotate the hydrant top operating nut counter-clockwise slowly until fully open (feel for the valve to hit its stop, then back off 1/4 turn to prevent sticking).
  • Pump operator opens the pump intake bleeder valve to exhaust air before opening the master intake valve.

    ---

⚠️ Common Pitfalls & Fireground Hazards
  • Stepping inside the hose loop: If the hose snags on the tailgate while the truck drives away, an internal loop will whip around your legs and cause severe fractures. Stay outside the loop!
  • Not flushing the hydrant: Rocks, sediment, and rust sitting inside the hydrant barrel will shoot into the fire pump, destroying the impeller and packing seals.
  • Closing the hydrant fast: Slamming a hydrant shut creates a catastrophic pressure wave (water hammer) that can rupture municipal mains under the street!
🗣️ 5-Minute Tailgate Debrief
  1. "Did the Storz lock snap closed properly?", "How long did it take between dismounting the truck and having the hydrant connected?"
✅ Apparatus Reset Checklist
  • Hydrant shut completely until operating nut stops; check barrel drainage (suction check).
  • Caps replaced and tightened hand-tight with wrench.
  • LDH drained: Walk the hose with a drainage roller or over-the-shoulder walk.
  • LDH repacked in the apparatus bed (flat load with folds slightly staggered).
  • Hydrant kit repacked and returned to apparatus compartment.
  • Attendance roster signed.
