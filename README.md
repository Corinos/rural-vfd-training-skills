## 🎯 The Purpose of This Skills Book
In a rural New Brunswick volunteer fire department, officers are frequently working shifts, out of town, or responding from other jobs when training night arrives. Too often, training nights stall, turn into truck washes, or get cancelled altogether simply because "no officer is here to run it."

**This skills book eliminates that problem.**

Every drill in this collection is designed to be **completely turnkey and self-contained on a double-sided sheet**:
* **PAGE 1 (FRONT):** Drill Title & NFPA Standard, Objective & Rural Value, Tools & Apparatus Checklist, Safety Officer "Watch Outs" & The Emergency STOP Rule, and Phase 1 Tailgate Walkthrough.
* **PAGE 2 (BACK):** Phase 2 Crew Repetitions & Coaching, Phase 3 Real-Time Scenario Challenge, Common Pitfalls, 5-Minute Tailgate Debrief, Apparatus Reset Checklist, and Training Record Sign-Off Block.

Any member—from a senior firefighter to a motivated six-month recruit—can pull a sheet from the binder, appoint a dedicated Safety Officer, gather the crew at the tailboard, and lead a safe, disciplined, 45-to-60-minute practical evolution that builds real muscle memory.

## 🌲 Tailored for Rural New Brunswick Realities
This curriculum is built from the ground up to address the actual operational challenges faced by New Brunswick rural departments:
* **Water Supply Without Hydrants:** Extensive coverage of static drafting, dry hydrants, frozen water sources, drop-tank shuttle setups, low-level strainers, and jet siphons to meet and maintain **Fire Underwriters Survey (FUS) Superior Tanker Shuttle Service (STSS)** benchmarks (saving community homeowners thousands on insurance).
* **WorkSafeNB Compliance (General Regulation 91-191):** Strict adherence to Section 51.6(2) companion/buddy requirements, two-in/two-out staffing rules (minimum 4 on scene before interior IDLH), personal jewellery prohibitions, and mandatory full PPE.
* **Winter & Maritime Hazards:** Chimney and wood stove fire procedures (creosote control, avoiding thermal shock down flues, TIC attic inspections, WETT clearance), cold-weather pump operations, and ice safety.
* **Firefighter Survival:** Standard Atlantic Canada emergency communications (**LUNAR** Mayday transmission), PASS management, GRAB LIVES, and reduced profile stud-breaching self-rescue.

## 📋 How to Set Up the Physical Station Binder
To get the most out of this collection, print and assemble it for the station:
1. **The 3-Ring Station Binder:** Place the printed sheets in a heavy-duty 2-inch waterproof binder kept on the dispatch desk in the radio room or the primary pumper cab.
2. **Double-Sided Printing:** Print the [**Complete Station Binder PDF**](file:///Users/scottmacdonald/repos/rural-vfd-training-skills/pdf/Rural_VFD_Weekly_Skills_Manual_Complete.pdf) selecting **"Print on both sides of paper" (Flip on long edge)**.
3. **Heavy-Duty Sheet Protectors:** Slide each double-sided drill sheet into a clear, heavy-duty plastic sleeve so it resists wet fireground conditions, mud, and greasy fingerprints.
4. **Laminated Pocket Cards:** Laminate [`QUICK_DRILL_LEADER_CARD.pdf`](file:///Users/scottmacdonald/repos/rural-vfd-training-skills/pdf/printable-cards/QUICK_DRILL_LEADER_CARD.pdf) and [`SAFETY_OFFICER_CHECKLIST.pdf`](file:///Users/scottmacdonald/repos/rural-vfd-training-skills/pdf/printable-cards/SAFETY_OFFICER_CHECKLIST.pdf) to keep clipped to the training clipboard.
5. **Attendance Roster:** Print 25 copies of [`ATTENDANCE_AND_SIGN_OFF_SHEET.pdf`](file:///Users/scottmacdonald/repos/rural-vfd-training-skills/pdf/ATTENDANCE_AND_SIGN_OFF_SHEET.pdf) in the front pocket to log training hours for provincial Fire Marshal (FCPNB) audits and insurance credit.

## 🏆 Standards & Accreditation References
* **NFPA 1001 / NFPA 1010:** Standard for Fire Fighter Professional Qualifications (Firefighter I & II).
* **Office of the Fire Marshal (OFM) New Brunswick:** Firefighter Certification Program of New Brunswick (FCPNB).
* **New Brunswick Community College (NBCC):** Fire Service Training Division (Miramichi Campus).
* **International Accreditation:** Dual accredited under **IFSAC** and **Pro Board**.
* **WorkSafeNB:** Occupational Health and Safety Act – *General Regulation 91-191* (Part VII.1 Firefighter Safety Equipment & Section 51.6(2) Companion/SCBA rules).
* **Fire Underwriters Survey (FUS):** Superior Tanker Shuttle Service (STSS) continuous water delivery accreditation.
