# 52-Week Seasonal Training Matrix
## Structured Weekly Training Plan for Rural New Brunswick Volunteer Fire Departments

> This matrix organizes weekly training night into 4 seasonal quarters aligned with Maritime weather conditions and fire call profiles. Each session references a 1-page skill sheet from this collection. Any member can pick up the designated sheet and run the drill.

---

### Quarter 1: Winter Operations & Firefighter Survival (January – March)
*Focus: Indoor bay skills, cold weather precautions, SCBA mastery, chimney fires, and firefighter survival.*

| **W04** | `20_Mayday_Calling_and_Firefighter_Survival.md` | NFPA 1001 Level 2 | Transmitting MAYDAY, LUNAR report, self-extrication from wire entanglement. |
| **W05** | `12_Primary_Search_Techniques.md` | NFPA 1001 Level 1 | Right-hand / left-hand search in blacked-out bay, tool-assisted oriented search. |
| **W06** | `03_SCBA_Cylinder_Replacement.md` | NFPA 1001 Level 1 | Solo and 2-person zero-visibility cylinder change, cascade refill system safety. |
