# Drill Safety Officer Checklist
## Dedicated Safety Role (Non-Participating Observer)

> **Mandate:** When an officer is not present, you are the department's front line against preventable injuries. You do **not** handle hoses, raise ladders, or swing tools during live reps. Your eyes stay 100% on the firefighters, the apparatus, and the training ground.

---

### 1. Pre-Drill Safety Scan (Before Reps Begin)
- [ ] **PPE Inspection:**
  * Bunker pants pulled over boots; suspenders secure.
  * Bunker coat fully zipped, velcro closed, collar turned up.
  * Nomex/PBI flash hood donned properly (no skin showing at neck or ears).
  * Helmet chinstrap fastened snugly under chin.
  * Structural firefighting or extrication gloves worn.
  * Safety glasses under visor/face shield.
- [ ] **WorkSafeNB Jewellery Rule:** Confirm no rings, wristwatches, or metal neck chains are worn under structural firefighting gear (NB General Regulation 91-191).
- [ ] **Ground Hazards:** Check apron/parking lot for:
  * Black ice / freezing water (scatter salt/sand immediately).
  * Trip hazards: uncoiled cords, loose cribbing, vehicle hitches, uneven curbs.
  * Traffic safety: If operating near the road, ensure traffic cones, high-vis vests, and emergency warning lights are active.

---

### 2. During the Drill (Active Surveillance)
- [ ] **Ergonomics & Lifting:** Watch for improper lifting (lifting with the back instead of legs during ladder carries, SCBA cylinder swaps, or hose rolls).
- [ ] **Pinch Points & Crush Zones:**
  * Ground ladder raises: fingers clear of rungs and bed section when dogs engage/disengage.
  * Hydraulic tools: hands positioned behind the safety guard, clear of tips/blades.
  * Vehicle stabilization: no body parts under unsupported loads.
- [ ] **Hose & Pressurized Lines:**
  * Ensure firefighters do not straddle uncharged hose lines before charging.
  * Warn members of nozzle "kick" when charging lines; ensure backup firefighter is anchored behind the nozzleman.
  * Verify pump discharge pressure matches drill parameters.
- [ ] **Air & Hydration (SCBA Drills):**
  * Check cylinder pressures (minimum 4,000 PSI before starting evolution).
  * Watch for signs of heat exhaustion, dizziness, or hyperventilation.
  * Ensure water/electrolytes are available at the tailboard.

---

### 3. The Emergency Stop Authority
You have absolute authority to stop any action instantly.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| IF YOU SEE ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:                                            |
|   1. A member falls, trips, or loses PPE (glove off, helmet dislodged)      |
|   2. A hose line whips or a coupling fails                                  |
|   3. Hydraulic tool slips from a purchase point                             |
|   4. Uncontrolled water runoff creating black ice in traffic or walk paths  |
|   5. Any member displays sudden exhaustion, confusion, or injury            |
|                                                                             |
| SHOUT LOUDLY: "STOP! STOP! STOP!" OR "FREEZE!"                              |
| EVERYONE MUST CEASE MOVEMENT, SHUT DOWN THROTTLES, AND STAND IN PLACE.      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### 4. Post-Drill Safety Clearance
- [ ] Ensure all members doff gear in a well-ventilated area (minimize cancer-causing particulate exposure).
- [ ] Wipe hands and neck with decontamination wipes.
- [ ] Verify all tools are inspected for damage, cracks, or hydraulic fluid leaks before being locked back in truck compartments.
- [ ] Confirm no injuries occurred. If any member sustained a minor cut, burn, or strain, report it immediately on the department First Aid / WorkSafeNB incident log.
