# Quick Drill Leader Pocket Card
## "I'm Running Training Tonight" (No Officer Required)

> **Keep this laminated card in the radio room or truck cab clipboard.** 
> If you are the senior firefighter—or the only one willing to step up—grab this card, grab a 1-page drill sheet, and follow these 5 steps. You don't need a lecture; you need hands on tools.

---

### Step 1: Gather the Crew & Set Roles (3 Minutes)
1. **Blow the station siren / ring the bell / call the room to order at 19:00 (7:00 PM).**
2. **Assign the Drill Safety Officer:**
   * Pick one member (rotate weekly).
   * Hand them the *Safety Officer Checklist*.
   * Remind them: *"Your only job is watching for hazards and calling STOP if something looks wrong. You don't do the physical reps while on safety watch."*
3. **Pick Tonight's Drill:**
   * Check the `ANNUAL_TRAINING_SCHEDULE.md` or pull the next sheet in the binder.
   * Read the **Objective**, **Real-World Rural Value**, and **Safety Warnings** aloud to the crew.

---

### Step 2: Tool Staging & Radio Check (7 Minutes)
1. Direct the crew to pull the specific tools and apparatus listed on the drill sheet.
2. Ensure full structural PPE is worn (WorkSafeNB Reg 91-191):
   * Bunker gear, boots, gloves, flash hood, helmet with chin strap DOWN, eye protection.
   * Rings, watches, and neck chains off.
3. Have all portable radios switched to local tactical channel (e.g., Tac 1 / Local Simplex). Perform a quick radio check.

---

### Step 3: Phase 1 – The Slow Walkthrough (10–15 Minutes)
1. Walk through the evolution step-by-step with the sheet in your hand.
2. Do **not** use a stopwatch. Do it at half speed.
3. Have an experienced member demonstrate while you read the steps.
4. Encourage questions: *"How does our truck differ from the textbook?"*, *"Where is this valve on Pumper 1 vs Tanker 2?"*

---

### Step 4: Phase 2 & 3 – Live Repetitions & Realistic Scenario (25–35 Minutes)
1. Split the crew into pairs or small teams (Buddy System).
2. Run 2–3 repetitions per crew so everyone gets hands-on time (not just the drivers or veterans).
3. If the drill includes Phase 3 (Challenge):
   * Add a realistic curveball: gloved hands only, darkened bay, simulated smoke (hoods reversed over mask), or timed continuous flow.
4. Remind everyone: **Anyone can yell "STOP!" if someone slips or gear fails.**

---

### Step 5: Tailgate Debrief & Truck Reset (10 Minutes)
1. Gather at the tailboard of the primary truck.
2. Ask the 3 Debrief Questions:
   * **"What went smooth and fast?"**
   * **"What snagged or slowed us down?"**
   * **"If this was 02:00 AM on a snowy back road, what would surprise us?"**
3. Execute the **Truck Reset Checklist** (Hose repacked, SCBA refilled to 4,500 PSI, water topped up).
4. Have everyone sign the **Training Sign-Off Sheet** to log provincial training hours.
